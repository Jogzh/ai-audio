The 13 MP3 recordings in this directory are copied without modification from
the Tone.js audio distribution. This project uses a single recording per sampled
pitch, repitches nearby notes, and changes playback gain/filtering to express
velocity; it does not include all 16 velocity layers of the original collection.

The playback bank uses the left microphone channel of each AB stereo recording,
preserving that recorded waveform instead of averaging phase-shifted microphones.
The engine adds gentle pan and room ambience. In the rendered composition the
recordings are repitched, enveloped, filtered
and mixed with independently written music and synthesized accompaniment.
The sample license is separate from the repository's software MIT license.
